ABOUT DJ STARKEY

LIONS DEN

Music for every party.
Energy for every moment.

DJ Starkey is a UK-born mobile DJ based in Tilburg, bringing professional DJ entertainment, great music and the right atmosphere to birthdays, weddings, private parties, corporate events and celebrations.

With a strong focus on EDM, House and Techno, DJ Starkey can also take your party through a wide range of sounds and eras. From 80s, 90s, 2000s and 2010s classics to today's biggest dance and party favourites, every set is tailored to the event, the guests and the atmosphere you want to create.

Whether you're after an energetic club-style night, a festival-inspired party, a nostalgic throwback or a mix of everything that gets people dancing, the music is shaped around your event.

DJ Starkey provides his own professional DJ equipment, sound system and lighting , creating a complete experience from the first track to the final beat.
